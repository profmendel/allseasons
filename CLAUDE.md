## Project Overview

This project is for a real catering business called **All Seasons Catering Company**, based in Nigeria.

The primary goal is to create a premium website that showcases the company's professionalism, food quality, previous events, and catering services, giving potential customers confidence to book them for their own events.

The website should feel luxurious, modern, trustworthy, and conversion-focused. Every page should be designed to make visitors think:

> "This is the catering company I want for my event."

The website is not just an online brochure.

It is also an intelligent quotation and booking platform that guides customers from discovering the company to requesting a quotation, confirming their booking, paying their deposit, and becoming a confirmed client.

The booking experience should replace long WhatsApp conversations and manual quotations with a beautiful guided experience.

---

# Business Goals

The platform should help All Seasons Catering Company:

- Showcase their brand professionally
- Display previous events and catering highlights
- Build trust with new customers
- Increase booking enquiries
- Reduce manual conversations
- Speed up quotation generation
- Organize customer bookings
- Track payments
- Manage upcoming events
- Deliver an exceptional customer experience

---

# Customer Journey

The website should guide visitors through the following journey.

## 1. Discover

The visitor lands on a beautiful homepage showcasing:

- Hero section
- Signature dishes
- Beautiful food photography
- Previous events
- Customer testimonials
- Awards or achievements
- Why choose All Seasons Catering Company
- Call-to-action

The objective is to build trust immediately.

---

## 2. Explore

The visitor can browse:

- About Us
- Catering Services
- Event Gallery
- Food Gallery
- Packages
- Testimonials
- Frequently Asked Questions

---

## 3. Request a Quote

When the customer is ready, they click **Request a Quote**.

They complete a guided booking form where they provide:

- Event details
- Guest count
- Event location
- Event date
- Preferred package
- Menu selections
- Soup selections
- Side dishes
- Extras
- Special requests

The experience should be simple, elegant, and enjoyable.

---

## 4. Receive a Professional Quote

The booking request is saved in Supabase.

The administrator reviews the request and prepares a professional quotation.

The customer receives a beautifully designed email with:

- Price breakdown
- Deposit required
- Quote expiry date
- Accept Quote button
- Decline Quote button

---

## 5. Confirm Booking

After accepting the quotation, the customer is shown:

- Bank details
- Deposit amount
- Payment instructions
- Upload payment receipt

The admin confirms payment.

The booking status changes to:

Confirmed.

# Catering Packages

Instead of hardcoding the packages into the website, they should be managed from the Admin Dashboard.

Initially create the following packages supplied by the client:

- Silver Package
- Gold Package
- Platinum Package (VVIP)

Each package should have:

- Hero image
- Description
- Included menu items
- Beautiful card design
- "Choose this Package" button

Future packages should be manageable without changing code.

---

# Admin Dashboard

The dashboard should allow the business owner to manage every aspect of the website without requiring a developer.

Whenever possible, content displayed on the website should come from the database instead of being hardcoded.

This includes:

- Hero banners
- Gallery
- Testimonials
- Packages
- Menu items
- Services
- Events
- Quotes
- Customers
- Bookings

The website should be designed so that the business can continue growing for many years without major redevelopment.
